Split — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy applies to the offline Android app Split (package com.mythos.split), published by its developer under the Split-Android project.

Data stored on your device

When you use Split, you may enter group and traveler names, expense titles, amounts, dates, notes, splits, advances, repayments and budgets. Split keeps these records in a private on-device SQLite database. Your selected appearance preference is stored locally.

Data collection and sharing

Split does not require an account, does not include ads or analytics, has no network permission, and does not upload records to the developer or a remote server. The developer does not receive your ledger through normal app use.

If you choose to export a full backup, CSV, PDF or share text, you select where the information goes using Android's system file picker or sharing interface. That destination or receiving app may handle data under its own privacy terms. Split does not automatically transmit those exports.

Security and backups

Android's private app storage protects Split's database under normal device security. Cloud backup of app storage is disabled. Exported JSON backups, PDFs, CSVs and shared text are not encrypted. Protect these files and avoid sending them to anyone you do not trust. The SHA-256 checksum in a backup detects accidental corruption, not unauthorized reading or tampering.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you remove them through the app, clear its storage in Android Settings or uninstall Split. Clearing data or uninstalling deletes the private database from that installation; files you exported to other locations remain there until you delete them separately. Split has no remote account or server-side record store and cannot restore an unbacked-up ledger.

Children and changes

Split is a general expense-tracking utility and is not designed for children. If its data practices change, this page and the in-app privacy disclosure will be updated before release of the changed behavior.

Questions and contact

You may contact the developer through the project's GitHub support and issue page. The developer must also provide working contact information on the Google Play store listing before publishing.
