# Split — Google Play draft store listing

**Name:** Split — Group Expenses

**Short description:** Track shared trip expenses, cash advances and balances offline.

**Full description:**

Travel together, spend together, and keep the numbers clear with Split.

Split helps track expenses for tours, trips, flatmates, shared meals and other group activities. Anyone in your group can pay; Split records how much each person paid, owes and should receive.

- Create groups and add travelers or participants.
- Record expenses and divide them equally or with custom amounts.
- Track who contributed to a payment.
- Record cash advances, repayments and settlement movements.
- See group totals, each person's expense share and outstanding balances.
- Use a dedicated overview and export a single-page settlement statement.
- Review a detailed ledger and export PDF or CSV reports.
- Save or restore full offline JSON backups.
- Choose light, dark or system appearance.

**Offline by design.** No account is needed, and expense records remain on your phone unless you choose to export or share them. Exported backup and report files are not encrypted.

Split tracks entered amounts and suggested transfers. It does not transfer money, connect to a bank or process payments.

**Suggested category:** Finance (review in Play Console)

**Proposed audiences:** General adult users and tour groups; verify age-targeting declaration.

**Release notes draft:** Updated Android compatibility and Google Play distribution packaging while retaining expense calculations, offline backups and PDF reports.
